Active Showcase: Real-Time Sign Language Recognition

Real-Time Isolated Sign Language Recognition Using Transformer-Based Models

Authors: AAIR Research Team • Year: 2026 • Track: Computer Vision • IEEE / CVF CVPRW

Isolated Sign Language Recognition across vast vocabularies demands nuanced spatial-temporal alignment capable of capturing rapid micro-finger articulations under unconstrained consumer camera angles. We present a novel unified framework that fuses 2D/3D joint coordinates via RTMPose with Spatial-Temporal Graph Convolutional Networks (ST-GCN) cascaded into a temporal Transformer Encoder, achieving 92.4% Top-1 accuracy with edge inference latency under 18.5ms.
